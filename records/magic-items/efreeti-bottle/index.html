Source text

Wondrous Item, Very Rare When you take a Magic action to remove the stopper of this painted brass bottle, a cloud of thick smoke flows out of it. At the end of your turn, the smoke disappears with a flash of harmless fire, and an Efreeti appears in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. The first time the bottle is opened, the GM rolls on the following table to determine what happens.
