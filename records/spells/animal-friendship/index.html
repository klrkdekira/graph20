Source text

Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Druid, Ranger) Casting Time: Action Range: 30 feet Components: V, S, M (a morsel of food) Duration: 24 hours Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Charmed condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spells ends. Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.
