Source text

Necromancy Cantrip (Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard) Casting Time: Action Range: 30 feet Components: V, S Duration: Instantaneous You spray toxic mist at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d12 Poison damage. Cantrip Upgrade. The damage increases by 1d12 when you reach levels 5 (2d12), 11 (3d12), and 17 (4d12).
