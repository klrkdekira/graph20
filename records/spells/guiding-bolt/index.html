Source text

Level 1 Evocation (Cleric) Casting Time: Action Range: 120 feet Components: V, S Duration: 1 round You hurl a bolt of light toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 4d6 Radiant damage, and the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn has Advantage. Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
