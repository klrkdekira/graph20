Source text

Level 3 Abjuration (Bard, Ranger, Wizard) Casting Time: Action Range: Touch Components: V, S, M (a pinch of diamond dust worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes) Duration: 8 hours For the duration, you hide a target that you touch from Divination spells. The target can be a willing creature, or it can be a place or an object no larger than 10 feet in any dimension. The target can't be targeted by any Divination spell or perceived through magical scrying sensors.
