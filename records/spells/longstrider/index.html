Source text

Level 1 Transmutation (Bard, Druid, Ranger, Wizard) Casting Time: Action Range: Touch Components: V, S, M (a pinch of dirt) Duration: 1 hour You touch a creature. The target's Speed increases by 10 feet until the spell ends. Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
