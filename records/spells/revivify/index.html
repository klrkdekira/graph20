Source text

Level 3 Necromancy (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger) Casting Time: Action Range: Touch Components: V, S, M (a diamond worth 300+ GP, which the spell consumes) Duration: Instantaneous You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. That creature revives with 1 Hit Point. This spell can't revive a creature that has died of old age, nor does it restore any missing body parts.
