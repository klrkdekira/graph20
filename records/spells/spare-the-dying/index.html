Necromancy Cantrip (Cleric, Druid) Casting Time: Action Range: 15 feet Components: V, S Duration: Instantaneous Choose a creature within range that has 0 Hit Points and isn't dead. The creature becomes Stable. Cantrip Upgrade. The range doubles when you reach levels 5 (30 feet), 11 (60 feet), and 17 (120 feet).
