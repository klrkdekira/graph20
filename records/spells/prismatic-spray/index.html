Source text

Level 7 Evocation (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard) Casting Time: Action Range: Self Components: V, S Duration: Instantaneous Eight rays of light flash from you in a 60-foot Cone. Each creature in the Cone makes a Dexterity saving throw. For each target, roll 1d8 to determine which color ray affects it, consulting the Prismatic Rays table. Prismatic Rays
