Level 8 Enchantment (Bard, Warlock) Casting Time: Action Range: Self Components: V Duration: 1 hour Until the spell ends, when you make a Charisma check, you can replace the number you roll with a 15. Additionally, no matter what you say, magic that would determine if you are telling the truth indicates that you are being truthful.
