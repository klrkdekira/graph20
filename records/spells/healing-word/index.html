Source text

Level 1 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid) Casting Time: Bonus Action Range: 60 feet Components: V Duration: Instantaneous A creature of your choice that you can see within range regains Hit Points equal to 2d4 plus your spellcasting ability modifier. Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The healing increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 1.
