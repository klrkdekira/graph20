Source text

Large Beast, Unaligned AC 15 Initiative +1 (11) HP 52 (7d10 + 14) Speed 40 ft. Senses Blindsight 60 ft.; Passive Perception 9 Languages None CR 3 (XP 700; PB +2) Actions Multiattack. The scorpion makes two Claw attacks and one Sting attack. Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) from one of two claws. Sting. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) Poison damage.
