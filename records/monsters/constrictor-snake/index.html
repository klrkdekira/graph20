Source text

Large Beast, Unaligned AC 13 Initiative +2 (12) HP 13 (2d10 + 2) Speed 30 ft., Swim 30 ft. Skills Perception +2, Stealth +4 Senses Blindsight 10 ft.; Passive Perception 12 Languages None CR 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2) Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage. Constrict. Strength Saving Throw: DC 12, one Medium or smaller creature the snake can see within 5 feet. Failure: 7 (3d4) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12).
