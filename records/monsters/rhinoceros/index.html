Source text

Large Beast, Unaligned AC 13 Initiative -1 (9) HP 45 (6d10 + 12) Speed 40 ft. Senses Passive Perception 11 Languages None CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2) Actions Gore. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage. If target is a Large or smaller creature and the rhinoceros moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) Piercing damage and has the Prone condition.
