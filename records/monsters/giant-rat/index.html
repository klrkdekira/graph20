Source text

Small Beast, Unaligned AC 13 Initiative +3 (13) HP 7 (2d6) Speed 30 ft., Climb 30 ft. Skills Perception +2 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 12 Languages None CR 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2) Traits Pack Tactics. The rat has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the rat's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition. Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 feet. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.
