Large Beast, Unaligned AC 11 Initiative +1 (11) HP 22 (3d10 + 6) Speed 40 ft., Climb 30 ft. Skills Perception +3 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 1 (XP 200; PB +2) Actions Multiattack. The bear makes one Bite attack and one Claw attack. Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage. Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.
