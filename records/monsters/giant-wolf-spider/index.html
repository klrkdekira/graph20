Medium Beast, Unaligned AC 13 Initiative +3 (13) HP 11 (2d8 + 2) Speed 40 ft., Climb 40 ft. Skills Perception +3, Stealth +7 Senses Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2) Traits Spider Climb. The spider can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check. Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Poison damage.
