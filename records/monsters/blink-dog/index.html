Medium Fey, Lawful Good AC 13 Initiative +3 (13) HP 22 (4d8 + 4) Speed 40 ft. Skills Perception +5, Stealth +5 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 15 Languages Blink Dog; understands Elvish and Sylvan but can't speak them CR 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2) Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage. Bonus Actions Teleport (Recharge 4–6). The dog teleports up to 40 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
