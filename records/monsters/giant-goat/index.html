Source text

Large Beast, Unaligned AC 11 Initiative +1 (11) HP 19 (3d10 + 3) Speed 40 ft., Climb 30 ft. Skills Perception +3 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2) Actions Ram. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the goat moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 5 (2d4) Bludgeoning damage and has the Prone condition.
