Source text

Large Dragon (Metallic), Chaotic Good AC 17 Initiative +4 (14) HP 119 (14d10 + 42) Speed 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 80 ft. Skills Deception +5, Perception +7, Stealth +4 Immunities Acid Senses Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft.; Passive Perception 17 Languages Common, Draconic CR 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3) Actions Multiattack. The dragon makes three Rend attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Slowing Breath. Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 10 ft. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) Slashing damage. Acid Breath (Recharge 5–6). Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 14, each creature in a 40-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. Failure: 40 (9d8) Acid damage. Success: Half damage. Slowing Breath. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. Failure: The target can't take Reactions; its Speed is halved; and it can take either an action or a Bonus Action on its turn, not both. This effect lasts until the end of its next turn.
