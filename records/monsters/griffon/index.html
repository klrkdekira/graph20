Large Monstrosity, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 59 (7d10 + 21) Speed 30 ft., Fly 80 ft. Skills Perception +5 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 15 Languages None CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2) Actions Multiattack. The griffon makes two Rend attacks. Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) from both of the griffon's front claws.
