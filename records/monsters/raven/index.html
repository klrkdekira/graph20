Tiny Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 2 (1d4) Speed 10 ft., Fly 50 ft. Skills Perception +3 Senses Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 0 (XP 10; PB +2) Traits Mimicry. The raven can mimic simple sounds it has heard, such as a whisper or chitter. A hearer can discern the sounds are imitations with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check. Actions Beak. Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1 Piercing damage.
