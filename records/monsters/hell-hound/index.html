Source text

Medium Fiend, Lawful Evil AC 15 Initiative +1 (11) HP 58 (9d8 + 18) Speed 50 ft. Skills Perception +5 Immunities Fire Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 15 Languages Understands Infernal but can't speak CR 3 (XP 700; PB +2) Traits Pack Tactics. The hound has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hound's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition. Actions Multiattack. The hound makes two Bite attacks. Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Fire damage. Fire Breath (Recharge 5–6). Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. Failure: 17 (5d6) Fire damage. Success: Half damage.
