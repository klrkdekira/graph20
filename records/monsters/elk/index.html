Large Beast, Unaligned AC 10 Initiative +0 (10) HP 11 (2d10) Speed 50 ft. Skills Perception +2 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 12 Languages None CR 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2) Actions Ram. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the elk moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage and has the Prone condition.
