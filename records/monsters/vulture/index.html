Medium Beast, Unaligned AC 10 Initiative +0 (10) HP 5 (1d8 + 1) Speed 10 ft., Fly 50 ft. Skills Perception +3 Senses Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 0 (XP 10; PB +2) Traits Pack Tactics. The vulture has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the vulture's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition. Actions Beak. Melee Attack Roll: +2, reach 5 ft. Hit: 2 (1d4) Piercing damage.
