Tiny Monstrosity, Unaligned AC 14 Initiative +2 (12) HP 5 (2d4) Speed 30 ft., Fly 60 ft., Swim 30 ft. Senses Blindsight 10 ft.; Passive Perception 11 Languages None CR 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2) Traits Flyby. The snake doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach. Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1 Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Poison damage.
