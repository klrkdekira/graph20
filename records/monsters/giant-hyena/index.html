Large Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 45 (6d10 + 12) Speed 50 ft. Skills Perception +3 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 1 (XP 200; PB +2) Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage. Bonus Actions Rampage (1/Day). Immediately after dealing damage to a creature that was already Bloodied, the hyena can move up to half its Speed, and it makes one Bite attack.
