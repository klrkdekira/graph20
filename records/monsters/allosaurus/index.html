Source text

Large Beast (Dinosaur), Unaligned AC 13 Initiative +1 (11) HP 51 (6d10 + 18) Speed 60 ft. Skills Perception +5 Senses Passive Perception 15 Languages None CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2) Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) Piercing damage. Claws. Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the allosaurus moved 30+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition, and the allosaurus can make one Bite attack against it.
