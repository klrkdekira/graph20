Large Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 45 (6d10 + 12) Speed 5 ft., Swim 40 ft. Skills Perception +2 Senses Blindsight 60 ft.; Passive Perception 12 Languages None CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2) Traits Water Breathing. The shark can breathe only underwater. Actions Bite.
