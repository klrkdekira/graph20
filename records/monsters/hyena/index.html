Source text

Medium Beast, Unaligned AC 11 Initiative +1 (11) HP 5 (1d8 + 1) Speed 50 ft. Skills Perception +3 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 0 (XP 10; PB +2) Traits Pack Tactics. The hyena has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hyena's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition. Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +2, reach 5 ft. Hit: 3 (1d6) Piercing damage.
