Large Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +1 (11) HP 19 (3d10 + 3) Speed 40 ft., Climb 40 ft. Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 10 Languages None CR 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2) Traits Spider Climb.
