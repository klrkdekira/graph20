Source text

Large Beast, Unaligned AC 13 Initiative +0 (10) HP 42 (5d10 + 15) Speed 40 ft. Senses Passive Perception 8 Languages None CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2) Traits Bloodied Fury. The boar has Advantage on melee attack rolls while it is Bloodied. Actions Gore. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the boar moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) Piercing damage and has the Prone condition.
