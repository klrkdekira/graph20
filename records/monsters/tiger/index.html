Source text

Large Beast, Unaligned AC 13 Initiative +3 (13) HP 30 (4d10 + 8) Speed 40 ft. Skills Perception +3, Stealth +7 Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 1 (XP 200; PB +2) Actions Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition. Bonus Actions Nimble Escape. The tiger takes the Disengage or Hide action.
