Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 7 (2d6) Speed 30 ft. Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage. Bonus Actions Nimble Escape. The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.
