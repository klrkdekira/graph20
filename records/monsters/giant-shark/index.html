Huge Beast, Unaligned AC 13 Initiative +3 (13) HP 92 (8d12 + 40) Speed 5 ft., Swim 60 ft. Skills Perception +3 Senses Blindsight 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13 Languages None CR 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3) Traits Water Breathing. The shark can breathe only underwater. Actions Multiattack. The shark makes two Bite attacks. Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +9 (with Advantage if the target doesn't have all its Hit Points), reach 5 ft. Hit: 22 (3d10 + 6) Piercing damage.
