Medium Beast, Unaligned AC 10 Initiative +0 (10) HP 11 (2d8 + 2) Speed 40 ft. Senses Passive Perception 10 Languages None CR 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2) Traits Beast of Burden. The mule counts as one size larger for the purpose of determining its carrying capacity. Actions Hooves. Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.
