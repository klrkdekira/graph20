Tiny Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +1 (11) HP 1 (1d4 - 1) Speed 5 ft., Swim 20 ft. Skills Perception +2, Stealth +5 Senses Passive Perception 12 Languages None CR 0 (XP 0; PB +2) Traits Water Breathing. The seahorse can breathe only underwater. Actions Bubble Dash. While underwater, the seahorse moves up to its Swim Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
