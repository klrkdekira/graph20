Large Dragon, Unaligned AC 14 Initiative +0 (10) HP 127 (15d10 + 45) Speed 30 ft., Fly 80 ft. Skills Perception +4 Senses Darkvision 120 ft.; Passive Perception 14 Languages None CR 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3) Actions Multiattack. The wyvern makes one Bite attack and one Sting attack. Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage. Sting. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 10 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 24 (7d6) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the start of the wyvern's next turn.
