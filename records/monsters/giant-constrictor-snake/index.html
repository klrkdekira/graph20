Source text

Huge Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 60 (8d12 + 8) Speed 30 ft., Swim 30 ft. Skills Perception +2 Senses Blindsight 10 ft.; Passive Perception 12 Languages None CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2) Actions Multiattack. The snake makes one Bite attack and uses Constrict. Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 10 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage. Constrict. Strength Saving Throw: DC 14, one Large or smaller creature the snake can see within 10 feet. Failure: 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14).
