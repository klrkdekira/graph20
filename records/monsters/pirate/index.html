Source text

Medium or Small Humanoid, Neutral AC 14 Initiative +5 (15) HP 33 (6d8 + 6) Speed 30 ft. Gear Daggers (6), Leather Armor Senses Passive Perception 11 Languages Common plus one other language CR 1 (XP 200; PB +2) Actions Multiattack. The pirate makes two Dagger attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Enthralling Panache. Dagger. Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage. Enthralling Panache. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 12, one creature the pirate can see within 30 feet. Failure: The target has the Charmed condition until the start of the pirate's next turn.
