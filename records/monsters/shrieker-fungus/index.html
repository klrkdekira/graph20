Medium Plant, Unaligned AC 5 Initiative -5 (5) HP 13 (3d8) Speed 5 ft. Immunities Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened Senses Blindsight 30 ft.; Passive Perception 6 Languages None CR 0 (XP 0; PB +2) Reactions Shriek.
