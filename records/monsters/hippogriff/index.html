Large Monstrosity, Unaligned AC 11 Initiative +1 (11) HP 26 (4d10 + 4) Speed 40 ft., Fly 60 ft. Skills Perception +5 Senses Passive Perception 15 Languages None CR 1 (XP 200; PB +2) Traits Flyby. The hippogriff doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach. Actions Multiattack. The hippogriff makes two Rend attacks. Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.
