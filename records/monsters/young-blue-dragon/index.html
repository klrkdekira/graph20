Large Dragon (Chromatic), Lawful Evil AC 18 Initiative +4 (14) HP 152 (16d10 + 64) Speed 40 ft., Burrow 20 ft., Fly 80 ft. Skills Perception +9, Stealth +4 Immunities Lightning Senses Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft.; Passive Perception 19 Languages Common, Draconic CR 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4) Actions Multiattack. The dragon makes three Rend attacks. Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 10 ft. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage plus 5 (1d10) Lightning damage. Lightning Breath (Recharge 5–6). Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. Failure: 55 (10d10) Lightning damage. Success: Half damage.
