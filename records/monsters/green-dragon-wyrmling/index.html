Source text

Medium Dragon (Chromatic), Lawful Evil AC 17 Initiative +3 (13) HP 38 (7d8 + 7) Speed 30 ft., Fly 60 ft., Swim 30 ft. Skills Perception +4, Stealth +3 Immunities Poison; Poisoned Senses Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 14 Languages Draconic CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2) Traits Amphibious. The dragon can breathe air and water. Actions Multiattack. The dragon makes two Rend attacks. Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d10 + 2) Slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage. Poison Breath (Recharge 5–6). Constitution Saving Throw: DC 11, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. Failure: 21 (6d6) Poison damage. Success: Half damage.
