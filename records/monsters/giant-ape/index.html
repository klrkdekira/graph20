Source text

Huge Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +5 (15) HP 168 (16d12 + 64) Speed 40 ft., Climb 40 ft. Skills Athletics +9, Perception +4, Survival +4 Senses Passive Perception 14 Languages None CR 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3) Actions Multiattack. The ape makes two Fist attacks. Fist. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 10 ft. Hit: 22 (3d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. Boulder Toss (Recharge 6). The ape hurls a boulder at a point it can see within 90 feet. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 17, each creature in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point. Failure: 24 (7d6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition. Success: Half damage only. Bonus Actions Leap. The ape jumps up to 30 feet by spending 10 feet of movement.
