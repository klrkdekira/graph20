Source text

Large Undead, Lawful Evil AC 13 Initiative +1 (11) HP 22 (3d10 + 6) Speed 60 ft. Vulnerabilities Bludgeoning Immunities Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned Senses Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 9 Languages None CR 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2) Actions Hooves. Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the skeleton moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition.
