Source text

Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 11 (2d8 + 2) Speed 10 ft., Fly 50 ft. Skills Perception +5 Resistances Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing Immunities Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned Senses Passive Perception 15 Languages None CR 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2) Traits Swarm. The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny raven. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points. Actions Beaks. Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied. Cacophony (Recharge 6). Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 10, one creature in the swarm's space. Failure: The target has the Deafened condition until the start of the swarm's next turn. While Deafened, the target also has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.
