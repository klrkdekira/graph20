Tiny Beast, Unaligned AC 12 Initiative +2 (12) HP 1 (1d4 - 1) Speed 20 ft., Climb 20 ft. Skills Stealth +4 Senses Darkvision 30 ft.; Passive Perception 10 Languages None CR 0 (XP 10; PB +2) Traits Spider Climb. The spider can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check. Web Walker. The spider ignores movement restrictions caused by webs, and the spider knows the location of any other creature in contact with the same web. Actions Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1 Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Poison damage.
