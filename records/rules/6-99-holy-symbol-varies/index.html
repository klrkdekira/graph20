Holy Symbol (Varies)

Rule

SRD 5.2.1 · Equipment §6.99 · Holy Symbol (Varies) · lines 6575–6587

Indexed facts

section
6.99

Source text

A Holy Symbol takes one of the forms in the Holy Symbol table and is be jeweled or painted to channel divine magic. A Cleric or Paladin can use a Holy Symbol as a Spellcasting Focus. The table indicates whether a Holy Symbol needs to be held, worn, or borne on fabric (such as a tab-ard or banner) or a Shield.
