Preparing Spells

Rule

SRD 5.2.1 · Spells §7.3 · Preparing Spells · lines 7062–7079

Indexed facts

section
7.3

Source text

If you have a list of level 1+ spells you prepare, your spellcasting feature specifies when you can change the list and the number of spells you can change, as summarized in the Spell Preparation by Class table. Most spellcasting monsters don't change their lists of prepared spells, but the GM is free to alter them.
