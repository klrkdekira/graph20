Controlling a Mount

Rule

SRD 5.2.1 · Playing the Game §1.83 · Controlling a Mount · lines 697–703

Indexed facts

section
1.83

Source text

You can control a mount only if it has been trained to accept a rider. Domesticated horses, mules, and similar creatures have such training. The Initiative of a controlled mount changes to match yours when you mount it. It moves on your turn as you direct it, and it has only three action options during that turn: Dash, Disengage, and Dodge. A controlled mount can move and act even on the turn that you mount it. In contrast, an independent mount - one that lets you ride but ignores your control - retains its place in the Initiative order and moves and acts as it likes.
