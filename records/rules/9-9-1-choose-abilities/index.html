9.9

Source text

Choose three abilities that seem appropriate for the background: Strength or Dexterity. These abilities are ideal for a background involving physical exertion. Constitution. This ability is ideal for a background that involves endurance or long hours of activity. Intelligence or Wisdom. One or both abilities are ideal for a background that focuses on cerebral or spiritual matters. Charisma. This ability is ideal for a background that involves performance or social interaction.
