Rolling 20 or 1

Rule

SRD 5.2.1 · Playing the Game §1.21 · Rolling 20 or 1 · lines 212–216

Indexed facts

section
1.21

Source text

If you roll a 20 on the d20 (called a “natural 20”) for an attack roll, the attack hits regardless of any modifiers or the target’s AC. This is called a Critical Hit (see “Combat” later in “Playing the Game”). If you roll a 1 on the d20 (a “natural 1”) for an attack roll, the attack misses regardless of any modifiers or the target’s AC.
