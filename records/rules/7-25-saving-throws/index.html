7.25

Source text

Many spells specify that a target makes a saving throw to avoid some or all of a spell's effects. The spell specifies the ability that the target uses for the save and what happens on a success or failure. Here's how to calculate the DC for your spells: Spell save DC = 8 + your spellcasting ability modifier + your Proficiency Bonus
