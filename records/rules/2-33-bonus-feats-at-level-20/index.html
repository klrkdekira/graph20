Bonus Feats at Level 20

Rule

SRD 5.2.1 · Character Creation §2.33 · Bonus Feats at Level 20 · lines 1254–1258

Indexed facts

section
2.33

Source text

A GM can use feats as a form of advancement after characters reach level 20 to provide greater power to characters who have no more levels to gain. With this approach, each character gains one feat of their choice for every 30,000 XP the character earns above 355,000 XP. Epic Boon feats are especially appropriate for these bonus feats, but a player can choose any feat for which their level 20 character qualifies. in the “Level Advancement” section. You begin with the minimum amount of XP required to reach your starting level. For example, if the GM starts you at level 10, you have 64,000 XP.
